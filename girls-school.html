Zaitoon Girls' School and College

A residential girls' school and college giving girls from remote villages a safe place to live, learn and complete their education.

The vision

A school and college built for girls

For a girl in a remote village, the nearest safe school can be an impossible distance away. Cost, distance, early marriage and safety keep too many girls out of the classroom — and the district leaves them furthest behind of all.

Zaitoon Girls' School and College will be a safe place for girls to study — from nursery all the way through to college. It will give them far more than lessons: a full education that equips them to build a better, brighter generation, alongside practical skills such as sewing that let them earn a little money of their own.

What it provides

Everything a girl needs to stay in education

Safe boarding

Secure, supervised accommodation so girls from far-off villages can live on site and study.

Full education

Schooling from nursery all the way through to college, all in one place.

Meals & care

Nourishing meals, pastoral care and a secure, supportive environment.

Teaching & materials

Qualified teaching staff, classrooms and the learning materials girls need to thrive.

A real future

A path forward for girls who might otherwise be married early or kept from school.

Skills to earn

Practical skills such as sewing, so girls can earn a little money of their own alongside their education.

The campaign

Building it together

The school is a live building project, rising in Siddharth Nagar. Every contribution helps put up classrooms and boarding accommodation, fit them out, and staff them — turning foundations into a place where girls can learn for generations.

  • Classrooms and safe boarding accommodation
  • Furnishing, equipment and learning materials
  • Teaching and pastoral staff

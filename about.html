Who we are

A community organisation working where help is needed most — and where too few others reach.

Our story

Founded in 2010, built on one belief

Zaitoon Educational & Welfare Society was officially established in 2010 by people who refused to accept that a child's future should be decided by their family's income.

Since then we have run educational projects and welfare work across Siddharth Nagar, always guided by the same conviction: that the way out of poverty and isolation is through learning. The name Zaitoon — the olive — stands for the peace, growth and resilience we want to see take root in this community.

The region we serve

Siddharth Nagar, Uttar Pradesh

To understand our work, it helps to understand where we work — and why so little help arrives here on its own.

An unwritten language

The everyday language of the area is a spoken dialect with no written form. Children grow up without the written mother tongue that most education takes for granted.

Schooling only the few can buy

The handful of schools that exist charge fees. Poor families cannot pay them, so the poorest children never enrol — and the cycle of poverty repeats.

No work close to home

There are no factories, and farmland has been divided down the generations until it can no longer support a family. Work means leaving.

Migration and exploitation

Families travel to Delhi, Mumbai and other cities for work. Without qualifications or the city's language, many end up exploited in the shanty towns — women and girls most of all.

A word of welcome

From our founder

How we work

Our approach

Reach the poorest first

We prioritise the children and families that fee-paying schools leave behind. Need, not means, decides who we serve.

Education that leads to work

We pair schooling with job-oriented and vocational training, so learning translates into a livelihood people can build at home.

Lift girls and women

We invest deliberately in girls and women, because their education changes the direction of a whole family and community.

Rooted in the community

We are run by and for the people of the area — accountable to the families we exist to serve.

Careful stewardship

We keep our operations lean and transparent so that support goes as far as it possibly can.

In it for the long term

Real change is generational. We are building institutions meant to outlast any single campaign.

Governance & transparency

Run responsibly

Zaitoon is a registered educational and welfare society governed by a committee drawn from the community it serves. We operate to a full set of safeguarding, child-protection and financial policies.

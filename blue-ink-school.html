Blue Ink School International

Our school in Siddharth Nagar — fee-free primary and secondary education, with a boarding facility so children from distant villages can stay and learn.

The school

Where it all begins

Blue Ink School International runs fee-free classes for children whose families are locked out of the district's fee-paying schools — from the early primary years right through secondary. It is the foundation of everything we do: the reading, writing and numeracy that every other opportunity depends on.

For children who grow up speaking an unwritten mother tongue, the school opens a written pathway into education — and a route out of the cycle of poverty and migration.

What we offer

A full education, open to all

Fee-free places

Need is the only entry requirement — no child is turned away because their family is poor.

Primary to secondary

A continuous education from the early years right through to secondary school.

Foundational skills

Reading, writing and numeracy — and a written pathway for children of an unwritten mother tongue.

Qualified teaching

Dedicated teachers, classrooms and the materials children need to learn.

A safe environment

A secure, supportive place where children can focus on learning.

A route forward

The education that makes work, further study and a better future possible.

Boarding facility

Boys' Boarding Facility

Many boys live too far from school to travel each day. Our boarding facility gives them a safe place to live, study and be cared for while they complete their education — so distance is no longer a reason to drop out.

  • Safe boarding accommodation for boys from distant villages
  • Meals, care and a secure place to study
  • A chance to stay in school instead of dropping out to migrate

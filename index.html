Our belief

We believe the solution to poverty and isolation lies in education and training — and that no child should be shut out of a classroom because their family is poor.

The challenge

A district the future forgot

Siddharth Nagar is one of the poorest districts of Uttar Pradesh — a scatter of small towns and villages where the local language exists only as a spoken dialect, never written down.

The few schools that exist charge fees that poor families simply cannot pay. With no factories and shrinking farmland, families migrate to cities like Delhi and Mumbai for work, where too many end up exploited in the shanty towns. For girls and women, the barriers are higher still.

What we do

Four ways we open doors

Free schooling

A proper education for children whose families could never afford school fees — no child turned away for being poor.

Girls & women

Safe, dedicated education for girls and women, who face the steepest barriers of all in the district.

Skills & trades

Job-oriented and vocational training so young people can earn a living without leaving for the cities.

Community welfare

Practical support for families in need, because learning takes root only when basic needs are met.

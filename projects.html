Zaitoon Girls' School and College

A residential girls' school and college bringing schooling and safe boarding together in one place, so girls from remote villages can live, learn and complete their education, from the early years right through college.

Learn more Fund the school

Primary & Secondary Schooling

Delivered through Blue Ink School International — fee-free classes from the early primary years through secondary, for children whose families are locked out of the district's fee-paying schools.

Boarding facility

Boys who live too far to travel each day can stay, study and be cared for in our on-site boarding facility.

Learn more Support this work

Skills & Vocational Training

Schooling matters most when it leads somewhere. We provide job-oriented and vocational training so young people can build a livelihood in their own community — instead of migrating to cities where they risk exploitation. Alongside their classes, students also take part in extra-curricular activities such as sewing.

  • Practical, in-demand trades and skills
  • Extra-curricular activities such as sewing alongside their classes
  • A route to earning at home, close to family
  • A defence against the cycle of migration and exploitation

Curriculum Books for Younger Classes

For our younger classes we write and print our own curriculum books — teaching brotherhood, humanity and everything a child needs to progress. As students grow older, they move on to the government CBSE board and its books.

  • Our own curriculum books for the younger classes
  • Lessons in brotherhood, humanity and all a child needs to progress
  • Older students follow the government CBSE board and books

Community Welfare

Learning takes root only when a family's basic needs are met. For those with no source of income, we provide ration packs and free meals throughout the year — so no child goes hungry and families can keep going.

  • Ration packs for families with no source of income
  • Free meals provided throughout the year
  • Support that keeps children fed and families going

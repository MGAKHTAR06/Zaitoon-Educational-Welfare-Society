The Girls' Boarding School

For a girl in a remote village, the nearest safe school can be an impossible distance away. Our flagship campaign is building a residential girls' school — a place where they can live, learn and be safe while they complete their education.

What your support builds:

  • Safe boarding accommodation and classrooms
  • Meals, care and a secure environment
  • Teaching staff and learning materials
  • A future for girls who would otherwise be married early or kept from school

Free Primary Schooling

This is where it all begins. We run fee-free classes for children whose families are locked out of the district's fee-paying schools — giving them the reading, writing and numeracy that every other opportunity depends on.

  • No fees — need is the only entry requirement
  • Foundational literacy and numeracy
  • A written pathway for children of an unwritten mother tongue
Active

Skills & Vocational Training

Schooling matters most when it leads somewhere. We provide job-oriented and vocational training so young people can build a livelihood in their own community — instead of migrating to cities where they risk exploitation.

  • Practical, in-demand trades and skills
  • A route to earning at home, close to family
  • A defence against the cycle of migration and exploitation
Active

Girls' Education

Girls face the steepest barriers to schooling in the district — distance, cost, early marriage and safety. We make sure girls can start, stay in and complete their education, and go further than the generation before them.

  • Schooling and support for girls of every age
  • Confidence, independence and the skills to go further
  • Change that carries into the next generation
